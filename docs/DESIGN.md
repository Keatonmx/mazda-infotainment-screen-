# 魂動 KODO design rules

The look: Japan × Mazda, anime title-card style, on a 7" 800×480 screen you glance at while driving.
Every app and the startup greeting follow these rules. The shared styles live in [`theme/kodo.css`](../theme/kodo.css).

## 1. Glanceable first

- One **hero value** per screen: the thing you'd look for in half a second (coolant, speed, fuel, miles to oil change).
  At least 60px, in the display font.
- At most three secondary values, 32px or more.
- Labels are at least 15px. Nothing smaller than 14px anywhere.

## 2. Color means something

| Meaning | Color | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Cold / not ready | ai indigo `#5b8def` | cold engine |
| Caution / in progress | kin gold `#d4a857` | warming up, oil due soon, fuel under ¼ |
| Good | matcha green `#79c267` | engine warm, oil fine, fuel OK |
| Danger | alert red `#ff4d5e` | overheating, oil overdue, fuel low |
| Brand (chrome only) | Soul Red `#c8102e` | ribbons, corner brackets, stamps, conquered states |

Soul Red decorates. It never colors a value that is fine.

## 3. Two typefaces, fixed roles

| Role | Font | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Display | Dela Gothic One (`KodoDisplay`) | hero numbers, Japanese titles and labels |
| Text | M PLUS Rounded 1c ExtraBold (`KodoRound`) | all English text: labels, sentences, hints |

No letter-spaced display font at small sizes. English labels are `KodoRound`, 15px, with a little tracking.

## 4. Night-friendly

The background is sumi black. Large light areas (speech bubbles) are washi `#f4ede1`, never pure white.
Decorations like the side kanji are dimmed so data stays the brightest thing on screen.

## 5. One ornament per zone

- Top-left: the slanted red **ribbon** with the page name (Japanese + English)
- Right edge: dim vertical **kanji**, the app's identity
- Background: faint **seigaiha** waves
- Cards: hairline border and red **corner brackets**

## 6. Controls are always visible

Bottom-right **hint bar**: knob icons plus what they do on this page, for example
"◯ ページ PAGE · ● 決定 SELECT". Controls are never only explained in a sentence.

## 7. Characters are pixel art

Akari and Kodo share one style: 48×48 pixel art shown at 5× (240×240), with a dark outline and a limited palette.
Sheets are generated by PixelLab (`tools/pixellab/`), or by the placeholder generators in `tools/`.

## Hardware limits (Opera Presto, about 2012)

No CSS variables, no flexbox, `-o-` prefixes on transforms and gradients, ES5 JavaScript only, no CSS animation.
Japanese text only renders if its characters are in the subset fonts: run `node tools/build-theme.js`
after changing text, and `python3 tools/check-theme-glyphs.py` to confirm.
